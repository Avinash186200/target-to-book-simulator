# Results

Synthetic data only, generated by `make bench` (`python -m targetbook.cli bench --out results`).
Development seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 choose the desk's schedule and bands; held-out seeds 101-110 (n = 10) are reported with those choices frozen; held-out results were never used for any choice (held-out sections first read after the third full run; a fourth run only added diagnostic fields; see docs/methodology.md).
Each seed is an independent 11-year panel (1 warm-up year, 10 evaluated years). Cells are `median [min, max]` across held-out seeds. Returns are annualised arithmetic excess returns, in %.
